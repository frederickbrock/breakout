# Feel brief: <moment>

> Copy to `briefs/<moment>.md`. Short is fine. The pm turns each brief into
> issues, and the levers below become their acceptance criteria, so give
> numbers wherever you can (a range is fine: "4–8 px").

## Moment

The single thing that happens on screen (e.g. "a ceramic brick breaks").

## What the player should feel

One or two sentences. ("A crisp, satisfying crack, never mushy.")

## References

Games, clips or sounds that nail it, and what part of them to copy.

## Concrete levers

The knobs and their target values. Delete the ones that don't apply; add
your own.

| Lever | Target |
|---|---|
| Screen shake (amplitude, duration) | e.g. 6 px, 80 ms |
| Hit-stop (frames or ms frozen on impact) | e.g. 40 ms |
| Particles (count, speed, lifetime) | e.g. 12–16, 300 ms |
| Easing / squash-and-stretch | e.g. ease-out-back, 10% squash |
| Colour / flash | e.g. white flash 1 frame |
| SFX (sound, pitch variation, volume) | e.g. ±8% pitch, 3 variants |

## How we'll know it's right

Observable checks the tester can do: a measurement, a side-by-side, a
recorded clip, "10 breaks in a row never sound identical".

## Out of scope

What this brief does not change.
