# Art style guide — Steelbreak

Canonical look for all `art` work in this repo. Owned by the art-director; changed
only with the user. Chosen 2026-09-27 over the Enochian painterly lane (archived in
`art/archive/style-enochian.md`). Source of truth for the look: `art/steelbreak_concept.png`
(annotated sheet) and `art/steelbreak_keyart.png`, both generated by `art/gen_concept.py`.

## Look

Sci-fi industrial hangar in deep space: a dark void framed by steel trusses and
girders, lit by a cold key light from the upper left and a warm bounce from the lower
right. The ball is a polished steel sphere; the paddle is a magnetic emitter (two metal
prongs with a cyan field between them, no solid surface); bricks are machined metal and
ceramic plates floating on containment beams. Clean, crisp, slightly glossy hard-surface
rendering: precise bevels, seams, bolts, specular edges, restrained glow only where
energy lives (emitter field, reactor core).

## Palette

Material codes from the concept sheet.

| role | hex | usage |
|---|---|---|
| hangar void | `#0a0e15` | backdrop, negative space, HUD area |
| structural steel | `#16202c` | trusses, girders, deck plates |
| polished ball | `#a9bacd` | ball body; specular white highlight + cool rim |
| emitter field | `#4fd8ff` | paddle field, arena bounds, HUD accents |
| cold key light | `#a9dcff` | upper-left 6500K light shaft, rim light |
| warm bounce | `#ffab4d` | lower-right 2700K fill, hazards, warm rim on ball |
| ceramic band | `#ff6a4d` | fired-ceramic brick accent |
| reactor core | `#b58cff` | reactor-core brick / power-up only |

### Brick classes

Shape and surface carry the read, not hue alone (face gradient top → mid → bottom, seam, glow):

| class | face | seam / glow | read |
|---|---|---|---|
| fired ceramic | `#ffdcc8` → `#ff7d58` → `#79261a` | `#ffc0a6` / `#ff6a4d` | glazed ceramic tile, brittle |
| titanium alloy | `#eef6ff` → `#9fb5ca` → `#2f3c4a` | `#d3e9ff` / `#7fc4ff` | brushed pale metal, bolted |
| tungsten plate | `#ffe6bd` → `#d69a4e` → `#4f3212` | `#ffd08a` / `#ffab4d` | heavy dark-gold plate, thick bevel |
| reactor core | `#f6ecff` → `#a98cf0` → `#331f5c` | `#dcc9ff` / `#b58cff` | violet energy seam that glows; the power-up brick |
| explosive plate | `#ffd0cc` → `#e0303a` → `#5a0f14` | `#ff9a92` / `#ff3b3b` | hot red plate, hazard chevrons in the bevel; redder than ceramic |
| regen alloy | `#dcffe6` → `#3fcf6e` → `#0f4a24` | `#9dffbe` / `#3dff7a` | green alloy with fine lattice/vein etching |
| shield glass | `#e6fdff` → `#5fe3f5` → `#0e4a57` | `#b8f6ff` / `#4fd8ff` | translucent tempered glass in a steel frame, hex-cell refraction |

Every class has an **intact** and a **cracked** state; the cracked state keeps the
silhouette and class colour and adds dark fracture lines plus a chipped corner.
All classes share one plate silhouette (sim-rdl.5.1 pick r1/v1). Shield glass cracks as a
shatter web.

## Resolution & scale

Smooth hard-surface rendering, not pixel art. Game sizes (from `src/main.rs`):
play area 900×650, brick 80×30, ball 15 px, paddle 120×20, power-up icon 24×24.
Author sprites at 2× (bricks, paddle, icon) or 4× (ball) and downscale with
`artgen process --size` so edges stay crisp. Rectangular sprites fill their box edge
to edge (bricks tile in a grid with a small gap drawn by the game, not the sprite).

Text never enters the artwork. HUD (score, lives) is Bevy text overlaid in the dark
top-left area.

## Prompt preamble

Verbatim block prepended to **every** prompt in this project:

```
Sci-fi industrial game art, clean hard-surface rendering: machined steel, bevelled
metal plates, bolts and seams, crisp specular edges, subtle glossy highlights.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.
```

For **sprites** append the isolation rule (see `art-pipeline` production constraints):

```
Single isolated object, centred, whole object inside frame, orthographic front view,
plain flat solid #00FF00 green background, no shadow on the background, no scenery,
no other objects, no text.
```

Use `#00FF00` green as the key colour (not magenta) because the reactor-core violet
and ceramic red sit too close to magenta. Exception: green sprites (the regen-alloy brick) key on flat
`#FF00FF` magenta instead. The hangar background is the one piece
that wants full scenery and no key colour.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Read at game scale first: check every sprite at 1× against `#0a0e15`.
- Keep the ball a sphere (hard key specular, cool rim, warm rim), never a flat disc.
- Build the paddle from separable parts: left prong, right prong, stretchable field.
- Keep the background under ~12% contrast behind the brick area and dark in the top-left HUD corner.
- Keep bricks strictly rectangular so they tile.

**Don't**
- No violet outside the reactor core / power-up.
- No cyan glow outside energy (paddle field, bounds, HUD) and the shield-glass brick.
- No painterly brushwork, no organic shapes, no fantasy motifs.
- No text, numbers, hit-count pips or logos baked into sprites (hit state is shown by the cracked variant).
- No lens flare, heavy bloom or busy scenery behind the bricks.
