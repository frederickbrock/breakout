v1: titanium cracked A
Sci-fi space-age game art in a graffiti line-art style: bold clean contour outlines
with a dark keyline and a second offset inner line in off-white (#e2dfd2), warm
orange (#d98c64) or muted teal (#5f8f8f); flat graffiti colour blocking with strong
value contrast; crisp hard white highlight strokes and edge shines; stylised cel-like
lighting. Smooth rounded steel forms and flowing contour lines. No spray paint: no
drips, overspray, mist, speckle, stencil fuzz, tags or lettering.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

Subject: ONE single game brick, one image, one plate only, no table, no other bricks. Wide 8:3 rounded-rectangle plate, front view, centred, filling most of the frame width; thick dark keyline, off-white inner outline, corner bolts, centre seam, hard highlight streak, flat cel colour blocks, same silhouette and line weight as the first reference. CRACKED state, and this matters: the damage must be CLEARLY VISIBLE at a tiny size (80x30 px): 4 to 6 BOLD dark keyline fracture lines (thick, not hairlines) crossing a large part of the face, each with a thin off-white highlight edge on one side, branching, plus one clearly chipped corner and one or two small chipped notches; roughly 20% of the plate's pixels should be crack and chip. It must look clearly more damaged than an intact plate yet still be a complete plate, clearly less damaged than a broken one. The damage amount should be like the tungsten cracked reference (last reference). No spray effects, no text, no numbers. Single isolated object, whole object inside frame, plain flat solid #00FF00 green background, no shadow on the background, no scenery. Class: titanium alloy, pale brushed steel-blue (#eef6ff to #9fb5ca to #2f3c4a). The first reference is the intact graffiti plate; the second is a weak earlier attempt (too little damage, do better).
