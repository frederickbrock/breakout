Sci-fi industrial game art, clean hard-surface rendering: machined steel, bevelled
metal plates, bolts and seams, crisp specular edges, subtle glossy highlights.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

Subject: the player's paddle — a magnetic emitter, not a solid bar. It is built from
two identical machined-steel prong housings, one at the far left and one at the far
right (mirror images of each other), each a short, thick, bevelled metal block with
a bolt or two and a small emitter nozzle facing inward. Between the two prongs there
is NO solid surface at all — only a glowing horizontal energy field bridging the gap,
a bright cyan-white bar (#4fd8ff core, #a9dcff hot centre) with a soft glow, like a
horizontal lightsaber blade or containment field. The whole shape is a long, low,
wide horizontal band (roughly 6:1 width:height), flat-topped and flat-bottomed.

Use in game: this whole assembly slides left and right at the bottom of the screen
to bounce a ball; the empty gap must clearly read as energy, not glass or plastic,
and the field must look like it could stretch wider without changing the prongs.

Single isolated object, centred, whole assembly inside frame, orthographic front
view, plain flat solid #00FF00 green background, no shadow on the background, no
scenery, no other objects, no text, no numbers, no logos.
