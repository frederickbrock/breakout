Render everything in the image in pure grayscale only — white, light gray, mid

Format: a single horizontal sprite sheet strip, exactly 4 equal square frames

no border, no frame numbers or labels, frames touching edge to edge with no gaps
or padding between them. Flat solid #00FF00 green background filling every
frame equally — no scenery, no ground plane, no shadow, no text.

Subject: a small shower of bright titanium metal spark specks (like a welding/

Frame 1: the spark specks are small, near-white hot, tightly grouped near the

Frame 2: sparks have shot slightly further outward, still bright, trails a
little longer.
Frame 3: sparks are further spread out, cooling — mid-gray rather than near
white, trails fading.
Frame 4: sparks are scattered furthest apart, smallest, dimmest dark-gray

Keep the specks crisp and metallic (small hard bright points with thin trails),
not soft or cloud-like.
