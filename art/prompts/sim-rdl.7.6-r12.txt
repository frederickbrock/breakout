This is a GREYSCALE PARTICLE-EFFECT SPRITE SHEET, not a coloured game object.
Render everything in the image in pure grayscale only — white, light gray, mid
gray, dark gray, black — with absolutely no color or hue anywhere (this artwork
will be colour-tinted later by the game engine). Style: clean sci-fi hard-surface
particle FX, crisp readable shapes, no painterly brushwork.

Format: a single horizontal sprite sheet strip, exactly 4 equal square frames
arranged left to right in one row (wide 4:1 aspect ratio, like a film strip).
Each frame's content is centred within its own square cell. No dividing lines,
no border, no frame numbers or labels, frames touching edge to edge with no gaps
or padding between them. Flat solid #00FF00 green background filling every
frame equally — no scenery, no ground plane, no shadow, no text.

Subject: a small burst of angular fired-ceramic shard fragments (2-4 sharp
broken chips), the "hit/break" particle effect for a ceramic brick. Animate
across the 4 frames, reading left to right as a short one-shot burst:
Frame 1: the shard fragments are small, bright, sharp-edged and tightly grouped
near the frame centre, as if the instant after impact.
Frame 2: fragments have moved slightly further apart, still bright and crisp.
Frame 3: fragments are more spread out, slightly smaller, a little softer/lighter
in tone (starting to fade).
Frame 4: fragments are scattered furthest apart, smallest, faintest and softest
(nearly faded to background), as the burst finishes dispersing.
Keep the shard silhouette sharp and angular (ceramic, not metal or glass) in
every frame.
