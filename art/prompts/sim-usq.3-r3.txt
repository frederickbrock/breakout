v1: charge A
Sci-fi space-age game art in a graffiti line-art style: bold clean contour outlines
with a dark keyline and a second offset inner line in off-white (#e2dfd2), warm
orange (#d98c64) or muted teal (#5f8f8f); flat graffiti colour blocking with strong
value contrast; crisp hard white highlight strokes and edge shines; stylised cel-like
lighting. Smooth rounded steel forms and flowing contour lines. No spray paint: no
drips, overspray, mist, speckle, stencil fuzz, tags or lettering.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

Subject: ONE complete static behaviour outline FRAME, a thin hollow rounded-rectangle ring on a WIDE landscape canvas of aspect 168:68 (about 2.5:1, like the second reference): the ring fills the whole canvas, its outer edge touching the left, right, top and bottom image edges, NOT a small shape floating in the middle. All four sides and corners visible. The entire CENTRE is empty and filled with the same flat background colour as the outside. Use EXACTLY the line weight and construction of the second reference (a breach frame): very thin ring, outer dark keyline, thin flat coloured band, one thin off-white (#e2dfd2) inner line, inner dark keyline, small rounded corners, no steel housing, no thick bevel, no glow, no heavy outer outline. The first reference gives this behaviour's motif and colour: keep them. No spray effects, no text, no numbers. Behaviour: charge. Band colour warm orange (#ff9a3d), NO cyan anywhere. Motif: four small outlined corner node brackets. Single isolated object, plain flat solid #00FF00 background everywhere outside AND inside the ring, no shadow, no scenery.
