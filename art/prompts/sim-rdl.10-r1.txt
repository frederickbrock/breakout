v1: L0 space A
Sci-fi space-age game art in a graffiti line-art style: bold clean contour outlines
with a dark keyline and a second offset inner line in off-white (#e2dfd2), warm
orange (#d98c64) or muted teal (#5f8f8f); flat graffiti colour blocking with strong
value contrast; crisp hard white highlight strokes and edge shines; stylised cel-like
lighting. Smooth rounded steel forms and flowing contour lines. No spray paint: no
drips, overspray, mist, speckle, stencil fuzz, tags or lettering.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

Deep-space background art for a game, in the graffiti line-art style: keyline contours around forms, flat colour bands, hard highlight strokes on edges, cel-like lighting from the upper left, NO spray paint, no photographic wisps, no blur, no soft airbrush. Palette: near-black void #0a0e15, rust and orange (#ffab4d family), cream, and dusty blue (#a9dcff family). NO strong violet, NO cyan glow, no pure saturated colours; everything dark, calm and low contrast (it sits behind a game), nothing resembling a ball, brick, circle pickup or glowing gameplay object, no text. PORTRAIT canvas, taller than wide (about 2:3). The first reference(s) are photographs for MOOD AND SHAPES ONLY (do not copy them, do not match their brightness); the last reference shows the line treatment to use. LAYER L0, vast space: a near-black void filled with a sparse star field drawn as small cross-shaped and round outlined sparkles of a few sizes, plus a few faint distant galaxies drawn as tiny outlined ellipses and spirals with cream/dusty-blue colour bands. Fully opaque, extremely calm, mostly empty dark space.
