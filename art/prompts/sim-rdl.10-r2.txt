v1: L1 clouds + glow core A
Sci-fi space-age game art in a graffiti line-art style: bold clean contour outlines
with a dark keyline and a second offset inner line in off-white (#e2dfd2), warm
orange (#d98c64) or muted teal (#5f8f8f); flat graffiti colour blocking with strong
value contrast; crisp hard white highlight strokes and edge shines; stylised cel-like
lighting. Smooth rounded steel forms and flowing contour lines. No spray paint: no
drips, overspray, mist, speckle, stencil fuzz, tags or lettering.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

Deep-space background art for a game, graffiti line-art style: bold keyline contours, FLAT colour bands, hard highlight strokes on edges, cel-like lighting from the upper left, NO spray paint, NO photographic texture, NO blur, NO soft airbrush gradients, no tiny dust speckle. Palette: near-black void #0a0e15, rust and orange (#ffab4d family), cream, dusty blue (#a9dcff family); a violet/magenta energy glow is allowed ONLY as flat dim muted bands (clearly dimmer and less saturated than a bright violet), no cyan. Dark, calm, low contrast overall (a game sits on top), nothing resembling a ball, brick or pickup, no text. PORTRAIT canvas, taller than wide (about 2:3), content filling the whole canvas edge to edge, no white margins. Reference images show only the graffiti line treatment / shapes to keep, nothing photographic. LAYER L1 (clouds with glow core). Layout: an energy nebula in the upper left with a bright core, a few large outlined cloud masses around it and down the left side, a big empty dark centre, and one dim outlined planet at the lower right cut off by the right edge. The nebula core is drawn as graffiti glow: stacked flat glow bands (cream centre, orange, muted violet outer band), hard highlight strokes, and a few OUTLINED forked lightning bolts (keyline plus bright inner line) arcing out through the cloud masses. Clouds have keyline contours and flat rust/cream/dusty-blue bands. No planet in this layer, plain dark void elsewhere, dimmer clouds lower down.
