Sci-fi industrial game art, clean hard-surface rendering: machined steel, bevelled
metal plates, bolts and seams, crisp specular edges, subtle glossy highlights.
Cold blue-white key light from the upper left (#a9dcff), warm amber bounce from the
lower right (#ffab4d). Dark hangar palette: void #0a0e15, structural steel #16202c,
polished steel #a9bacd, cyan energy #4fd8ff used only for energy fields. Restrained
glow, high readability at small size, strong silhouette. Absolutely no text,
lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

Subject: one rectangular sci-fi arcade brick plate — establishing the single shared
plate shape and bevel profile that four material classes (fired ceramic, titanium
alloy, tungsten plate, reactor core) will later reuse with different face colours.
This round renders that shared shape using the titanium-alloy treatment: brushed
pale metal face, vertical gradient from near-white (#eef6ff) at the top through
pale blue-grey (#9fb5ca) at the middle to dark blue-grey (#2f3c4a) at the bottom,
a pale seam line (#d3e9ff) with a faint cool-cyan accent glow (#7fc4ff) along one
edge, bolted corners, a crisp hard bevel around the whole perimeter.

Use in game: one brick among a 10x6 grid in a Breakout-style arcade game. The plate
must be strictly rectangular, wider than tall (roughly 8:3 aspect ratio), filling
its bounding box edge to edge with only a small bevel radius at the corners (no
large rounded corners, no gaps), so it can tile edge-to-edge with identical bricks.
Intact state only (no cracks, no damage).

Single isolated object, centred, whole object inside frame, orthographic front
view, plain flat solid #00FF00 green background, no shadow on the background, no
scenery, no other objects, no text, no numbers, no hit-count pips, no logos.
